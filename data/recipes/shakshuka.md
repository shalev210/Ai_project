# Shakshuka

Servings: 4
Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 25 minutes
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, breakfast, israeli, one-pan
Allergens: eggs

## Ingredients
- 30 g olive oil
- 150 g onion
- 150 g red bell pepper
- 10 g garlic
- 800 g canned crushed tomatoes
- 15 g tomato paste
- 4 g sweet paprika
- 2 g ground cumin
- 6 g salt
- 300 g eggs (6 large eggs)
- 10 g fresh parsley

## Method
1. Heat the olive oil in a wide 28 cm skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onion and red pepper and cook for 8 minutes until soft.
2. Add the minced garlic, paprika and cumin and stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Stir in the tomato paste, then the crushed tomatoes and salt. Simmer uncovered for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens.
4. Make 6 wells with a spoon and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook on low heat for 5 to 7 minutes, until the whites are set but the yolks are still runny.
5. Sprinkle with chopped parsley and serve straight from the pan with fresh bread or pita.

## Tips and Variations
- For a spicy version add one chopped green chili or 1 teaspoon of harissa with the garlic.
- Green shakshuka: replace the tomatoes with 400 g spinach, 200 g chard and 100 ml cream.
- Crumble 100 g feta over the top during the last 2 minutes.
- If the sauce is too acidic add a pinch of sugar.

## Storage
The tomato sauce keeps 3 days in the fridge and freezes well for 2 months. Cook the eggs fresh when reheating; cooked eggs do not reheat well.
