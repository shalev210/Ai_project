# Chicken Schnitzel

Servings: 4
Prep time: 20 minutes
Cook time: 15 minutes
Tags: meat, kids, israeli, fried, dairy-free
Allergens: eggs, gluten, sesame

## Ingredients
- 600 g chicken breast
- 100 g eggs (2 large eggs)
- 60 g all-purpose flour
- 150 g breadcrumbs
- 20 g sesame seeds
- 6 g salt
- 2 g black pepper
- 2 g sweet paprika
- 120 g vegetable oil (for frying; about 40 g is absorbed)

## Method
1. Slice each chicken breast horizontally into 2 thin cutlets, cover with plastic wrap and pound to an even 1 cm thickness.
2. Set up three plates: flour seasoned with salt and pepper; beaten eggs; breadcrumbs mixed with sesame seeds and paprika.
3. Coat each cutlet in flour, shake off the excess, dip in egg and press firmly into the breadcrumbs.
4. Heat 1 cm of oil in a frying pan to 175°C. Fry the cutlets 3 minutes per side until deep golden.
5. Drain on a rack or paper towels. The internal temperature of the chicken must reach 74°C (165°F).

## Tips and Variations
- Oven version: brush the breaded cutlets with 2 tablespoons of oil and bake at 220°C for 18 minutes, flipping halfway. This saves roughly 70% of the frying oil.
- For gluten-free schnitzel use cornflake crumbs and rice flour.
- Rest breaded cutlets 10 minutes in the fridge before frying so the coating sticks.

## Storage
Refrigerate up to 3 days. Reheat in the oven at 200°C for 8 minutes to keep it crispy; the microwave makes the coating soggy.
