# Red Lentil Soup

Servings: 6
Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 30 minutes
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, soup, high-protein, budget, freezer-friendly
Allergens: none

## Ingredients
- 30 g olive oil
- 150 g onion
- 120 g carrot
- 100 g celery
- 10 g garlic
- 300 g red lentils
- 1500 g vegetable stock
- 4 g ground cumin
- 2 g turmeric
- 6 g salt
- 30 g lemon juice

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Add diced onion, carrot and celery and sweat for 8 minutes over medium heat.
2. Add garlic, cumin and turmeric and stir for 1 minute.
3. Rinse the red lentils until the water runs clear, add them with the stock and bring to a boil.
4. Reduce the heat and simmer partly covered for 20 minutes, until the lentils fall apart.
5. Blend with an immersion blender until smooth. Season with salt and lemon juice.

## Tips and Variations
- Red lentils do not need soaking, unlike green or brown lentils.
- Stir in 200 ml coconut milk at the end for a creamy, richer soup.
- Garnish with toasted cumin seeds, chili flakes and fresh coriander.

## Storage
Keeps 5 days in the fridge and up to 3 months in the freezer. The soup thickens as it cools; loosen it with a little water when reheating.
