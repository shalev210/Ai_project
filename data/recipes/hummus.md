# Classic Hummus

Servings: 6
Prep time: 15 minutes (plus overnight soaking)
Cook time: 60 minutes
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dip, israeli, middle-eastern
Allergens: sesame

## Ingredients
- 250 g dried chickpeas
- 5 g baking soda
- 150 g tahini
- 45 g lemon juice
- 8 g garlic
- 6 g salt
- 100 g ice water
- 20 g olive oil
- 2 g ground cumin

## Method
1. Soak the dried chickpeas overnight in plenty of cold water with half of the baking soda. They will double in size.
2. Drain, cover with fresh water, add the remaining baking soda and simmer for 50 to 60 minutes until very soft and the skins fall off. Overcooked chickpeas make the smoothest hummus.
3. Blend the tahini, lemon juice, garlic and salt in a food processor for 1 minute.
4. Add the warm drained chickpeas and process for 3 minutes. With the motor running pour in the ice water slowly until creamy and light.
5. Taste and adjust lemon and salt. Serve in a shallow bowl with olive oil, cumin and a few whole chickpeas on top.

## Tips and Variations
- Ice water is the secret to a fluffy, pale hummus.
- Canned chickpeas work in a hurry: use 2 cans (480 g drained) and simmer them for 15 minutes with baking soda.
- Serve warm with ful (fava beans) or a hard-boiled egg for a hummus plate.

## Storage
Keeps 4 days in an airtight container in the fridge. Cover the surface with a thin layer of olive oil to stop it drying out.
