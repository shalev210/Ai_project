# Shabbat Challah

Servings: 12 (2 loaves)
Prep time: 30 minutes plus 2.5 hours rising
Cook time: 30 minutes
Tags: bread, baking, vegetarian, shabbat, dairy-free
Allergens: gluten, eggs

## Ingredients
- 1000 g bread flour
- 14 g instant yeast
- 120 g sugar
- 15 g salt
- 150 g eggs (3 large eggs)
- 120 g vegetable oil
- 400 g water
- 50 g egg (1 egg for the glaze)
- 10 g sesame seeds

## Method
1. Mix the flour, yeast and sugar in a large bowl. Add the eggs, oil and lukewarm water (about 37°C), then the salt.
2. Knead for 10 minutes by hand or 7 minutes in a stand mixer until smooth and elastic.
3. Cover and let rise in a warm place for 1.5 hours until doubled.
4. Divide into 2 halves, divide each into 3 strands and braid. Place on lined baking trays.
5. Let rise for another 45 to 60 minutes. Brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
6. Bake at 180°C for 25 to 30 minutes until deep golden and the bottom sounds hollow when tapped.

## Tips and Variations
- Do not put salt directly on the yeast; it slows fermentation.
- For a sweeter loaf add 100 g raisins after kneading.
- Halve all quantities for a single loaf.

## Storage
Keeps 2 days wrapped at room temperature. Freeze baked loaves for up to 3 months and use leftovers for French toast.
