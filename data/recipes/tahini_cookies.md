# Tahini Cookies

Servings: 24 cookies
Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 12 minutes
Tags: baking, dessert, vegan, dairy-free, israeli
Allergens: gluten, sesame

## Ingredients
- 250 g all-purpose flour
- 120 g sugar
- 180 g tahini
- 100 g vegetable oil
- 5 g baking powder
- 1 g salt
- 5 g vanilla extract

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and line two trays with baking paper.
2. Mix all ingredients by hand into a soft, crumbly dough that holds together when pressed.
3. Roll into 2.5 cm balls (about 25 g each), place 3 cm apart and press lightly with a fork.
4. Bake 10 to 12 minutes until pale golden at the edges. They harden as they cool.

## Tips and Variations
- Use raw (unroasted) tahini for a lighter flavor; roasted tahini gives a deeper, nuttier taste.
- Roll the balls in sesame seeds or press an almond on top before baking.

## Storage
Keeps 2 weeks in an airtight container at room temperature.
