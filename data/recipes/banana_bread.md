# Banana Bread

Servings: 10 slices
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 55 minutes
Tags: baking, vegetarian, dessert, breakfast
Allergens: gluten, eggs, dairy

## Ingredients
- 350 g ripe bananas (3 large)
- 100 g butter
- 130 g brown sugar
- 100 g eggs (2 large eggs)
- 5 g vanilla extract
- 5 g baking soda
- 2 g salt
- 220 g all-purpose flour
- 60 g walnuts

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and line a 23 x 13 cm loaf pan with baking paper.
2. Mash the bananas with a fork. Mix in the melted butter, sugar, eggs and vanilla.
3. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture, then fold in the flour until just combined. Do not over-mix.
4. Fold in the chopped walnuts. Pour into the pan.
5. Bake 50 to 60 minutes, until a skewer in the center comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes in the pan.

## Tips and Variations
- The browner the bananas, the sweeter and more flavorful the bread.
- Dairy-free: replace the butter with 90 g of vegetable oil.
- Swap walnuts for 80 g chocolate chips.

## Storage
Keeps 3 days wrapped at room temperature or 1 week in the fridge. Freeze individual slices for up to 3 months.
