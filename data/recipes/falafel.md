# Falafel

Servings: 6 (about 30 balls)
Prep time: 25 minutes plus overnight soaking
Cook time: 20 minutes
Tags: vegan, middle-eastern, israeli, street-food, fried
Allergens: sesame (if served with tahini)

## Ingredients
- 400 g dried chickpeas
- 120 g onion
- 15 g garlic
- 40 g fresh parsley
- 30 g fresh coriander
- 6 g ground cumin
- 4 g ground coriander
- 9 g salt
- 5 g baking powder
- 30 g all-purpose flour
- 500 g vegetable oil (for frying; about 60 g is absorbed)

## Method
1. Soak the dried chickpeas for 18 to 24 hours. Never use canned or cooked chickpeas, the falafel will fall apart.
2. Drain well and grind with the onion, garlic and herbs in a food processor to a coarse, sand-like texture.
3. Mix in the spices, salt, baking powder and flour. Refrigerate the mixture for 30 minutes.
4. Shape into balls of about 30 g each.
5. Deep-fry in oil heated to 175°C for 3 to 4 minutes until dark golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels.

## Tips and Variations
- Test-fry one ball first; if it breaks apart add 1 more tablespoon of flour.
- Air-fryer: spray with oil and cook at 200°C for 12 minutes, shaking halfway.
- Serve in pita with hummus, tahini, chopped salad and pickles.

## Storage
Raw falafel mixture freezes very well for 3 months. Fried falafel is best eaten fresh; reheat in the oven at 200°C for 6 minutes.
