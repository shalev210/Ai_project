# Kitchen Safety and Substitutions Guide

Tags: guide, food-safety, substitutions, storage

## Safe Internal Temperatures
- Poultry (chicken, turkey), whole or ground: 74°C / 165°F.
- Ground beef, lamb and pork: 71°C / 160°F.
- Whole cuts of beef, lamb and pork: 63°C / 145°F, followed by a 3-minute rest.
- Fish: 63°C / 145°F, or until the flesh is opaque and flakes easily.
- Egg dishes (quiche, casseroles): 71°C / 160°F.
- Leftovers and reheated dishes: 74°C / 165°F.
Use an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part of the food, away from bone.

## Fridge and Freezer Rules
- Keep the fridge at 4°C or below and the freezer at -18°C.
- Refrigerate cooked food within 2 hours of cooking (1 hour if the room is above 32°C).
- Thaw frozen meat in the fridge, never on the counter. Allow about 24 hours per 2 kg.
- Cooked leftovers are generally safe for 3 to 4 days in the fridge.

## Common Substitutions
- 1 egg (in baking): 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed + 3 tablespoons water, rested 5 minutes; or 60 g mashed banana.
- 1 cup buttermilk: 1 cup milk + 1 tablespoon lemon juice or vinegar, rested 5 minutes.
- 100 g butter (in baking): 80 g vegetable oil.
- Self-raising flour: 150 g all-purpose flour + 1.5 teaspoons baking powder + a pinch of salt.
- Fresh herbs to dried: use one third of the amount (1 tablespoon fresh = 1 teaspoon dried).
- Sour cream: the same amount of thick Greek yogurt.

## Kosher Kitchen Notes
- Meat and dairy are not cooked or eaten together; schnitzel uses oil, not butter, for this reason.
- Pareve recipes (no meat and no dairy), such as hummus, falafel, challah and tahini cookies, can be served with either.

## Allergen Basics
- The 9 major allergens: milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy and sesame.
- Tahini, hummus and many breads contain sesame; always check labels.
