# Israeli Chopped Salad

Servings: 4
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 0 minutes
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, salad, raw, quick, low-calorie
Allergens: none

## Ingredients
- 400 g tomatoes
- 400 g cucumber
- 80 g red onion
- 15 g fresh parsley
- 30 g olive oil
- 30 g lemon juice
- 4 g salt
- 1 g black pepper

## Method
1. Dice the tomatoes and cucumbers into small, even 0.5 cm cubes. Finely chop the onion and parsley.
2. Place in a bowl and dress with olive oil, lemon juice, salt and pepper just before serving.

## Tips and Variations
- Salt only right before serving; salt draws water out of the vegetables and makes the salad soggy.
- Add diced red pepper, radish, or a spoon of sumac for variation.
- For a full meal add 200 g of cooked chickpeas or crumbled feta.

## Storage
Undressed chopped vegetables keep 1 day in the fridge. Once dressed, eat within a few hours.
