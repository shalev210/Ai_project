# Pasta al Pomodoro

Servings: 4
Prep time: 5 minutes
Cook time: 25 minutes
Tags: vegetarian, italian, pasta, quick, kids
Allergens: gluten, dairy

## Ingredients
- 400 g dry spaghetti
- 40 g olive oil
- 10 g garlic
- 800 g canned whole tomatoes
- 6 g salt
- 10 g fresh basil
- 40 g parmesan cheese

## Method
1. Warm the olive oil over low heat and gently fry the sliced garlic for 2 minutes without browning.
2. Add the tomatoes, crush them with a spoon, season with salt and simmer 20 minutes.
3. Meanwhile cook the spaghetti in well-salted boiling water (10 g salt per liter) until 1 minute before al dente.
4. Transfer the pasta to the sauce with half a cup of pasta water and toss over the heat for 1 minute.
5. Finish with torn basil leaves, a drizzle of olive oil and grated parmesan.

## Tips and Variations
- Pasta water contains starch that binds the sauce to the pasta; always save some before draining.
- Vegan: skip the parmesan or use nutritional yeast.
- Gluten-free: use rice or corn spaghetti and cook 1 minute less than the packet says.

## Storage
The sauce keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen. Cooked pasta is best eaten fresh.
